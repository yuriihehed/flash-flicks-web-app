Memory Palaces & Visualization

Memory palaces involve associating information with specific locations in a familiar space. This technique is used by memory champions for complex memorization tasks.

Best for: Visual learners and those needing to memorize large amounts of information.

Leitner System

The Leitner System enhances retention by sorting flashcards into different "boxes" based on how well you remember them.

Best for: Learners who struggle with time management and need a structured, efficiency-driven study method.

Spaced Repetition (SRS)

Spaced repetition is a scientifically proven technique that involves reviewing flashcards at increasing intervals over time.

Best for: Students preparing for long-term knowledge retention.
